3. Agreement Not to Disclose 
 
(a) Prashanth Kadasi hereby agrees that he / she shall hold in confidence and hereby agrees

and / or its clients and customers, Prashanth Kadasi undertakes to use at least the same 
degree of care in safeguarding the confidential information as he / she uses or would use

to protect the confidential information or intellectual property from unauthorized or 
inadvertent disclosure. 
(c) Prashanth Kadasi shall keep confidential all Personal Data and other confidential 
information.  Employee / Consultant / Intern / Others having access to or being involved 
with the processing of personal data of Black Piano and / or its clients (i) undertakes a

6. Non-Compete 
 
Employee / Consultant / Intern / Others inclusive of his / her direct beneficiaries in business, 
interest and title in recognition of the transfer of Confidential and Proprietary Information to 
Black Piano and / or its clients and customers hereby agrees not to directly or indirectly join 
the clients and / or compete with the business of Black Piano and / or its clients and customers 
and its successors and assigns during the term of the agreement and for a period of 2 years 
[Two Years] following the expiration or termination of this contract and notwithstanding the 
cause or reason for termination. 
 
7. Non-Solicitation 
 
Employee / Consultant / Intern / Others inclusive of his / her direct beneficiaries in 
business, interest and title in recognition of the transfer of Confidential and Proprietary 
Information to Black Piano and / or its clients and customers hereby agrees not to directly or 
indirectly initiate communication with a motive of soliciting business or services with Black 
Piano’s Clients or Prospective Clients for a period of 2 years [Two Years] following the 
expiration or termination of this contract and notwithstanding the cause or reason for 
termination. 
 
8. Non-Poaching 
 
Employee / Consultant / Intern / Others inclusive of his / her direct beneficiaries in business, 
interest and title in recognition of the transfer of Confidential and Proprietary Information to 
Black Piano and / or its clients and customers hereby agrees not to directly or indirectly initiate 
communication with anyone employed by Black Piano with a motive of poaching/recruiting 
employees of Black Piano for a period of 2 years [Two Years] following the expiration or 
termination of this contract and notwithstanding the cause or reason for termination.  
 
9. Non-Disparagement 
 
Employee / Consultant / Intern / Others inclusive of his / her direct beneficiaries in business, 
interest and title hereby agrees to refrain from any disparagement, defamation, libel, or 
slander of Black Piano or any of the clients of Black Piano.

a court of competent jurisdiction at the District and Session Court, Pune India.

12. Severability 
 
(a) The provisions of this agreement shall be deemed severable, and the unenforceability of any

13. Indemnity 
 
The Recipient shall indemnify each Indemnified Person fully and effectively from and against 
all demands, claims, suits, losses, liabilities, damages, costs and expenses which may be made 
or brought against that Indemnified Person, or which that Indemnified Person may suffer or

obligations under this Non-Disclosure Agreement. 
 
14. Force Majeure 
 
Neither party will be responsible for any failure to perform its obligations under this 
agreement due to causes beyond its control, including but not limited to acts of God, war, riot, 
embargoes, acts of civil or military authorities, fire, floods, or accidents.

All notices and communications required or permitted under this agreement shall be in writing
