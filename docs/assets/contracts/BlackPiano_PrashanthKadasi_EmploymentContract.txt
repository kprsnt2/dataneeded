Dear Prashanth, 
We are pleased to appoint you to the position of Data Analyst with Black Piano 
Technologies Pvt. Ltd. on the following terms and conditions: 
 
1. Commencement of Employment 
Your employment will be effective, as of 1-March-2026 
 
2. Salary and Expenses 
a. Your total annual remuneration is INR 12,10,000/- (Rupees Twelve Lakhs Ten 
Thousand only) per annum pro rata. Please refer to the Offer Letter for detailed 
description. 
b. We will pay your salary monthly on of the last working day of each month in respect 
of the whole calendar month by transfer into an Indian bank account of your 
choice.  
c. We normally review salaries once per year. However, there is no right to a review or 
to an increase.  
d. We will reimburse all reasonable business expenses as long as they are supported 
by receipts and reasonably incurred by you in the proper performance of your 
duties. 
e. Any payments due from you to us may be deducted from your salary and from any 
other money due to you from us (or from a Group Company) in the case of 
overpayment. 
 
3. Mode and Hours of Operation 
You will be required to contribute for at least 9 hours a day (excluding breaks) and the mode 
of operation will be virtual office until further notice. You will be required to visit our offices 
or our client’s office as per requirement from time to time. 
 
4. Timings 
a. The company’s official timings are from 9:00 am to 6:00 pm.  
b. The Company reserves the right to change your working days and hours and the 
employees should adhere with it. Employees deployed at Client site would adhere 
the client specific timings.  
c. In case where client is in a different time zone, client may request to adjust your 
work timings based to suit their requirements 
d. You may be required to work additional hours, including hours at weekends or 
during public holidays, whenever this is reasonably necessary to carry out your 
duties properly. This has already been taken into account in determining your salary 
and benefits and you will not be entitled to extra pay or time off if you work 
additional hours.  
e. You must submit your weekly timesheets as per prevailing process in the company. 
 
5. Leave/Holidays 
a. You will be entitled for 8 days of casual leave for every calendar year. CL will be 
prorated for those joining during the course of the year.  
b. You shall be eligible for 18 days of Earned Leave for every calendar year. EL will be 
prorated for those joining during the course of the year.

8. Remote working 
a. You will maintain a stable and reliable internet connection during working hours. 
b. You will arrange for an internet plan from a reputed internet services provider. Such

c. You will maintain an alternate internet connection incase of any failures with your 
primary service provider.  
d. You will also maintain a reliable power backup solution, such as a generator, UPS

of power failures on work commitments.  
e. In the event of any issues with internet connectivity or power outage or any other 
incident that impacts your availability or productivity you will promptly notify Black 
Piano Management and your client and take necessary steps to resolve the issue 
expediently. 
f. You will install all Black Piano and Client mandated communications apps on your 
mobile phone. 
g. You will inform Black Piano Management of any change in address or primary 
work location. 
 
9. Basis of Appointment 
It is understood that our decision to appoint you is based on the information given by you 
in the Bio-data/Application and during the interview you had with us. If at a later stage, it is 
found that the information you had provided is false, your services are liable to be 
terminated. 
 
10. Borrowing/Accepting Gifts 
You are not authorized to borrow or accept any money, gift, reward or compensation for 
your personal gains from / or otherwise place yourself under pecuniary obligation to any 
person/client with whom you may be having official dealings.

11. Probation Period 
a. There is no probation period. 
 
12. Termination 
a. Your appointment may be terminated by the Company, for any reason, by giving 
you not less than 60 (sixty) calendar days prior notice in writing or gross salary in 
lieu thereof.  
b. If at any time in our opinion, which is final in this matter you are found non - 
performer or guilty of fraud, dishonest, disobedience, disorderly behaviour, 
negligence, indiscipline, absence from duty without permission or any other 
conduct considered by us deterrent to our interest or of violation of one or more 
terms of this letter, your services may be terminated without notice and on account 
of reason of any of the acts or omission the company shall be entitled to recover 
the damages from you. 
c. The Company reserves the right to terminate your employment summarily without 
any notice period or termination payment, if it has reasonable ground to believe 
you are guilty of misconduct or negligence or have committed any fundamental 
breach of contract or caused any loss to the Company.  
d. You may terminate your employment with the Company, without any cause, by 
giving not less than 60 (sixty) calendar days (as per the clauses mentioned below) 
prior notice or gross salary for the mentioned duration, subject to the approval of 
the company. 
e. It will be at the discretion of the company to relieve you prior to the completion of 
the notice period and is non-negotiable 
f. On termination of your employment for whatever reason, you will return to the 
Company all property; documents and paper, both original and copies thereof, 
including any samples, literature, contracts, records, lists, drawings, blueprints, 
letters, notes, data and the like; and Confidential Information, in your possession or 
under your control relating to your employment or to clients’ business affairs. You 
must also inform us of any computer passwords used by you in the course of your 
employment or any passwords of which you are otherwise aware. We may withhold 
payment of your final salary or any other payment due or outstanding upon 
termination of your employment until you have fully complied with your 
obligations to return property, submit timesheets and reveal passwords. 
 
13. Confidential Information 
a. During your employment with the Company, you will devote your whole time, 
attention and skill to the best of your ability for its business. You shall not, directly 
or indirectly, engage or associate yourself with, be connected, concerned, 
employed or engaged in any other business or activities or any other position or 
work part time or pursue any course of study whatsoever, without the prior 
permission of the Company. 
b. You must always maintain the highest degree of confidentiality and keep as 
confidential the records, documents and other Confidential Information relating to 
the business of the Company which may be known to you or confided in you by 
any means, and you will use such records, documents and information only in a 
duly authorized manner in the interest of the Company. For the purposes of this 
clause ‘Confidential Information’ means information about the Company ’s 
business and that of its customers which is not available to the general public and 
which may be learnt by you in the course of your employment. This includes, but is 
not limited to, information relating to the organization, its customer list, 
employment policies, personnel, and information about the Company’s products, 
processes including ideas, concepts, projections, technology, manuals, drawing, 
designs, specifications, and all papers, resumes, records and other documents 
containing such Confidential Information.

14. Intellectual Property 
a. You acknowledge and agree that any work created or developed by you (whether 
alone or jointly) during your employment will belong to us if it:  
i. Is capable of exploitation by us in the normal course of our business; or

15. Health and Safety 
In accordance with health and safety legislation, you must:  
 
a. Take reasonable care for the health and safety of yourself and other persons who 
may be affected by your acts or omissions; 
b. Cooperate with us to enable us to ensure so far as is reasonably practicable the 
health, safety and welfare at work of all our employees and to comply with any 
other duties or requirements relating to health and safety; and 
c. Not interfere with, or misuse anything provided by us, in the interests of health, 
safety or welfare.  
 
16. Grievances, Disciplinary Issues and Suspension 
a. If you have a grievance relating to your employment, you should raise this in the 
first instance with your Line Manager.  
b. We may suspend you for however long we consider appropriate to investigate any 
aspect of your performance or conduct or to follow disciplinary proceedings. We 
may attach conditions to any such suspension. You must comply with any such 
conditions and cooperate fully with any investigation. During any period of 
suspension, you would normally receive the same pay and benefits as if you were 
at work, although we reserve the right to withdraw and/or defer pay and/or benefits 
in appropriate circumstances.

a. In the following clauses: 
i. “Client” means any Person who at any time during the period of 12

ii. “Prospective Client” means any Person who at any time during the 
period of 12 months immediately before the Termination Date had 
relevant discussions in which you were materially involved, for which you 
had responsibilities or about which you obtained or otherwise received 
confidential information.  
iii. “Person” means individual, firm, company, association, corporation or 
other organization.  
iv. “Termination Date” means the date of termination of your employment 
with us.

b. Non-Solicitation: 
i. This clause prevents employees approaching the Company’s Clients or 
Prospective Clients after they leave.  
ii. You agree that for a period of 12 months after the termination of your 
employment you shall not solicit business from any Client or Prospective 
Client of the Company with whom you had contact during the 12 months 
prior to the termination of your employment.  
c. Non-poaching:  
i. This clause prevents the employee poaching other members of staff after 
he or she has left the Company. This clause prevents an employee 
poaching other members of staff for a period of 12 months after 
termination.  
ii. You agree that you shall not for a period of 12 months after termination 
of your employment whether as principal or agent or employer or 
otherwise, whether directly or indirectly, recruit or try to recruit any 
person as an employee or consultant or in some other capacity if that 
person was at any time during the last 12 months of your employment 
employed by the Company and you had regular contact with him/her 
through you employment with the Company.  
d. You undertake that:  
i. If you receive an offer of employment, engagement or solicitation with a 
Person other than us, either during your employment or during the 
period for which the restrictions set out above remain in force, you will 
immediately provide that Person with a complete copy of this cluse and 
relevant definitions; and  
ii. If you accept the offer, you will immediately notify us of the identify of the 
Person and your acceptance of the offer.  
e. You agree that we are entering into the above restrictions and all relevant 
definitions for our own benefit and as a trustee for each Relevant Group Company.  
 
18. Applicability of Company Policy 
The Company shall be entitled to make policy declarations from time to time pertaining to 
matters like leave entitlement, maternity leave, employees ’ benefits, working hours, 
transfer policies, etc., and may alter the same from time to time at its sole discretion. All 
such policy decisions of the Company shall be binding on you and shall override this 
Agreement to that extent. 
 
19. Applicability of Client’s Policy 
In addition to the Company policies, while working on a client assignment, you will be 
required to adhere to your client’s policies related to delivery processes and confidentiality. 
 
20. Personal Information 
a. You consent to our processing personal information about you where this is 
necessary for reasonable business purposes including (without limitation) sharing 
appropriate information on a confidential basis with third party benefits providers 
and payroll operators. This may involve processing sensitive personal data about 
you including details relating to your health, ethnicity and any criminal record.  
b. You will keep us informed of any change in your residential address and telephone 
numbers or civil status. 
c. In addition, you consent to our using images of you as part of our sales, marketing 
and publicity collateral during and/or after your employment with the Company.  
 
21. Monitoring 
We monitor our premises and the use of our communication facilities and IT equipment.

22. Authority 
If any Letter of Authority or Power of Attorney is issued to you, you will undertake to return 
it on demand or on separation from the Company. 
 
23. Medical Examination 
a. The company has the right to get you medically examined at any time during the 
employment in the company and your future employment will depend upon your 
being found fit. You may avail leave due to you with the prior sanction of the 
management.  
b. Even in case of leave on medical grounds, you will have to inform the management

24. Manner of Conduct 
You shall, throughout the service of the company conduct yourself in a manner befitting a 
responsible member of the staff and in aligment to Black Piano’s Values: Honest, 
Courageous, Principled, Inspired. In case your conduct and/or behavior is found to be  
undesirable, the company reserves the right to terminate your services forthwith without 
any compensation, notice or salary in lieu of notice. 
 
25. Outside Employment and Interests 
During your employment you must not, without our written permission, hold office in, be 
employed by, been engaged in or by, or have any direct or indirect interest in, any other 
business or organization (other than as a shareholder of up to 3% of its issued shares for the 
purposes of investment only).  
 
26. Changes to Terms and Conditions 
The above terms and conditions are liable to be changed without any reference to you. You 
will always be governed by the relevant rules of the company applicable to the staff of your 
category, which are in existence or may be modified from time to time.    
 
27. Retirement
