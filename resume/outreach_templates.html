Outreach Templates

Copy-paste-ready templates for cold outreach. Generic and reusable — fill in the five placeholders per role (see §5).

Ground rules: every number below traces to the reconciled metrics in config/profile.md (2026-10-11). Lead with what a client or team actually used, not with a keyword list. Nothing here is auto-sent; a human copies, personalises, and sends.

5. How to personalise

Swap two or three tokens per role and leave the rest alone. The templates are deliberately generic in the middle so that only these change:

TokenWhat to put thereWhere to find it
{{company}}The company you're writing toThe posting
{{role}}The exact job title from the postingThe posting
{{name}}The recruiter or hiring manager's first nameLinkedIn / the posting
{{keyword}}One skill or tool the posting emphasises that you genuinely haveThe requirements section
{{keyword_sentence}}One sentence connecting that keyword to real work you didYour own history

Guidance:

  • One keyword, not five. Pick the single requirement you can back with a

production example. {{keyword_sentence}} exists so you say what you did with it, not that you know it.

  • Only claim what you actually used. If the posting wants Tableau and you

have used it, say so. If it wants dbt and you have not, leave it out — the resume carries the real stack and an interview will find the gap anyway.

  • Do not touch the numbers. They are the verified set. If a number is not in

config/profile.md, it does not go in the message.

  • Keep the projects labelled as projects. The independent work

(JobAgents, AgentSwarm, BugAgents, AI Eco, mSeat, BrandXY) is self-directed, not client or employer work. Say so, as the templates do.

6. Never say this

Never writeWhy
"Staff / Principal / Founding Engineer", "Head of Data"The real titles are Data Analyst at both Black Piano and Pi Software. There is no seniority to inflate.
"Agentic AI Systems Engineer" as a held job titleNot an employment title. It may describe a direction, never a job held.
"My pipeline serves 10,000 users" / "drove $X revenue" / "grew adoption"There are no production users, revenue, customers, or adoption figures. None.
"My findings were disclosed / accepted / paid / resolved"BugAgents findings are submission-ready drafts. Nothing has been submitted and no bounty exists.
Naming the BugAgents vendor, program, asset, or vulnerability classWithheld pending responsible disclosure. Cite the project and severity only, or omit entirely.
"My paper was peer-reviewed" / "published in a journal"Paper 1 is a Zenodo preprint (DOI 10.5281/zenodo.23232189) — not peer-reviewed, not a journal. Paper 2 is a manuscript, not submitted.
"I authored the cosmology papers"The agent authors are A001_DarkMatter / A002_QuantumCosmos. You built the system that wrote them; you are not the paper author.
Any number not in config/profile.mdSuperseded figures are actively misleading. config/profile.md carries a binding "Stale values — do not use" list; treat every number there as deleted. If it is not in the current metrics table, it does not go in the message.
Any "enterprise dashboards" countSuperseded wording. The verified figure is 10+ client dashboards at Pi Software.
Any BugAgents report count above the promoted totalSuperseded. BugAgents promoted 2 reports from 8 findings; the remainder were filter refusals.
A wall of tools with no outcomeKeyword soup reads as a template. One tool, one outcome, per sentence.
"Dear Sir/Madam", "To Whom It May Concern"Use a real name, or "Hi {{company}} hiring team".
Claims about clients you cannot nameClient confidentiality. Describe the work, not the client list.
