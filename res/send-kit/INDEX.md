## How to reach a hiring team when there is no public address

Verified: 1,517 live ATS postings across MongoDB / Databricks / GitLab
contained **zero** recruiter email addresses. Guessing
`first.last@company.com` gets you a bounce at best and a bad impression at
worst. Use one of these instead, in order:

1. **Apply through the link** (Option A in each file). This is what the
   recruiting team actually monitors, and your form answers are pre-written.
2. **LinkedIn:** search the company + "talent acquisition" + "India" or
   "recruiter", then connect with a 300-character note. Those two notes are
   in `LINKEDIN.md` next to this file.
3. **The company careers page / events page** for a named coordinator.
